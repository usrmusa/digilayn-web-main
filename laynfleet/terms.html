Rider Agreement

Terms for LaynRider Users

1. Booking & Fare Payment

Before confirming a booking, you see the fare calculated from verified road-route distance, the fleet's active distance-based pricing curve, minimum fare, and applicable return-trip pricing. The curve means fare is not calculated simply by multiplying distance by a fixed rate per kilometre. Changes to the curve steepness require driver voting.

The displayed fare and pricing settings are locked when you confirm. Subsequent fleet pricing updates do not alter an already-confirmed booking. By confirming, you agree to pay the driver upon trip completion in cash or by another agreed direct payment method.

2. Return Trips & Excess Waiting Charges

For return trips, the agreed waiting duration and the rate for exceeding it are clearly displayed before booking confirmation. If you exceed the agreed waiting time, an additional charge is accumulated using the locked waiting rate. The app displays this accumulating fee while the driver waits. You agree to pay the confirmed fare plus this excess charge upon return completion. Drivers may not add any arbitrary off-app markups.

3. Pickup Location & Accuracy

With permission, LaynRider requests location when needed for foreground service-area checks or when you select "Use Current Location" for a pickup. It does not track riders in the background. Pickup and destination coordinates selected for a booking are stored with that trip. You are responsible for ensuring your pickup pin is accurate and situated in a safe, accessible area for the driver to stop.

4. Passenger Conduct & Safety

Riders must treat drivers and their vehicles with care and respect. Damaging a driver's vehicle, carrying illegal substances, or engaging in harassing or aggressive behaviour will result in immediate permanent account suspension across all Digilayn platforms.

5. Community Use & Assumption of Risk

LaynRider is a connection platform for the Poortjie community, not a transport carrier. Digilayn does not employ drivers and does not verify the real identity of any rider or driver — names, photos, and phone numbers are self-provided and unverified. You use LaynRider at your own risk and are solely responsible for deciding whom to ride with; you should only accept rides from drivers you personally know and trust. Digilayn is not liable for user conduct or events occurring during a trip.

6. Cancellations

If you must cancel a ride, please cancel promptly through the app before the driver arrives to avoid unnecessary travel and fuel costs for the driver.

Driver Agreement

Terms for LaynDriver Partners

1. Independent Fleet Operator Status

Drivers operate as independent transport providers. Digilayn provides technology software and is not an employer or transport carrier. You retain full control over when you choose to go online, go offline, and accept ride requests.

2. Licensing, Roadworthiness & Legal Compliance

You represent and warrant that you hold a valid driver's license (with appropriate Professional Driving Permit / PrDP if applicable), that your vehicle is fully licensed and roadworthy, and that you comply with all South African National Road Traffic laws.

3. Live Location Sharing

While toggled to "Online" or during an assigned booking, LaynDriver requires background and foreground location access. This allows the dispatch radar to broadcast your position to nearby riders and compute accurate pickup routes and ETAs.

4. Democratic Fare Governance

Digilayn introduced the distance-based pricing curve. Eligible drivers vote on proposed fleet pricing changes for their vehicle type, including changes to the curve steepness. Approved pricing changes apply to new bookings; already-confirmed bookings retain their original fare and pricing settings. Drivers must charge only the confirmed fare plus the disclosed excess-waiting charge where applicable, and may not add off-app markups.

5. Road Liability & Disclaimers

Digilayn is not liable for traffic violations, road accidents, vehicle wear and tear, or third-party claims arising from vehicle operations. Drivers are required to maintain appropriate motor vehicle insurance.
