LaynFleet Privacy Policy

This policy explains transport-specific data practices, live GPS tracking, and rider-driver information sharing across the LaynRider passenger app and LaynDriver partner app under POPIA.

Last updated: November 16, 2026 • Published by Musa Mgijima trading as Digilayn • Republic of South Africa.

🔗

Digilayn Platform Privacy Addendum

LaynFleet is powered by the unified Digilayn backend. Your universal account credentials, profile custody, strict 18+ eligibility, security safeguards, and statutory rights are governed by the parent Digilayn Privacy Policy under POPIA. This Addendum details the operational data practices specific to ride dispatch, real-time location sharing, and trip records.

🛡️ Transport Privacy Highlights

  • ✓ No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade trip history or personal information to third parties or advertisers.
  • ✓ Rider Location Privacy: Riders are never tracked in the background. Location is requested in the foreground solely when checking service-area availability or setting a pickup pin.
  • ✓ Driver Live Location: Drivers broadcast live location while toggled "Online" or executing assigned trips so riders and dispatch radar can monitor vehicle arrivals.
  • ✓ Clear Erasure Rules: Riders can permanently delete their account and trip history via the account-deletion page. Driver profile deletion removes personal profiles while retaining historical dispatch logs for accounting.

⚠️ Poortjie Community Platform — Used at Your Own Risk

  • • LaynFleet is built for the Poortjie community, where riders and drivers largely know one another to arrange local trips.
  • • We do not verify the real identity of any user. Names, photos, and phone numbers are self-provided and unverified against official government registers.
  • • Riders and drivers use LaynFleet at their own risk. You should only accept or offer rides to and from people you personally know and trust.

1. Information Collected in LaynFleet

🧑 LaynRider

Rider Information

  • Account Identity: Display name, registered email, profile photo, and mobile phone number (stored to allow matched drivers to contact you; not verified via SMS).
  • Trip & Booking Data: Requested pickup location, destination, road-route distance, fare quotation, ride status, and timestamps.
  • On-Demand Foreground Location: With permission, LaynRider requests location when needed in the foreground to check service-area coverage or set a pickup pin. It does not run background rider tracking.
  • Ratings & Feedback: Voluntary ratings (1–5 stars) and optional feedback submitted after completed trips.
🚗 LaynDriver

Driver Information

  • Driver Profile & Vehicle: Full name, phone number, vehicle details (make, model, colour, plate registration), and documentation status.
  • Live Location Tracking (Foreground & Background): While toggled to "Online" or executing an active trip, LaynDriver collects continuous GPS location to broadcast vehicle availability on radar and calculate accurate arrival times.
  • Fleet Performance: Trip completion records, rider ratings, acceptance rates, and voting records on fleet pricing curve adjustments.
📱 Technical Infrastructure

Device & Telemetry Data

  • Device Registry: Recording active device sessions to keep accounts secure and allow single-device preferences.
  • Push Tokens: Cryptographic tokens from Firebase Cloud Messaging (or Huawei Push) to deliver trip dispatch alerts.
  • Maps & SDK Telemetry: Google Maps and Places SDKs (or Huawei Map Kit) collect technical telemetry, IP addresses, and interaction metrics necessary to render maps and route navigation.

2. Data Sharing Between Riders & Drivers

When a rider and driver are matched for a trip, personal details are shared exclusively to coordinate pickup and execution:

  • The driver sees the rider's name, profile photo, rating, contact phone number, and pickup location pin, and can call the rider directly from the app.
  • The rider sees the driver's name, profile photo, vehicle make, model, colour, registration plate number, live GPS location, and phone number, and can call the driver directly.

Riders can also view driver profiles and community ratings before booking. Submitted driver reviews and star ratings are visible within the platform to assist community transparency.

📁

3. Transport Data Retention & Trip Records

Universal account deletion is managed centrally under the parent Digilayn Privacy Policy. When a user account is deleted or when you stop using LaynFleet, transport-specific records are handled as follows:

Rider Trip Data

When a rider account is deleted through the universal Digilayn deletion process, rider-created bookings and associated trip pickup/dropoff logs are permanently removed from active databases.

Driver Booking History & Audit Logs

When a driver profile is deleted, personal driver details and vehicle registrations are permanently removed. However, completed trip records, dispatch timestamps, and fare logs are retained unchanged in an immutable audit state for dispatch accounting, road safety compliance, and financial verification.

Universal Account Deletion

To permanently delete your universal Digilayn Account and erase your personal data across all connected platforms, see Section 6 of the Digilayn Privacy Policy.
