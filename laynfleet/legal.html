Rider Agreement

Terms for LaynRider Users

1. Booking & Fare Payment

Before confirming a booking, you see the fare calculated from verified road-route distance, the fleet's active distance-based pricing curve, minimum fare, and applicable return-trip pricing. The curve means fare is not calculated simply by multiplying distance by a fixed rate per kilometre. Changes to the curve steepness require driver voting.

The displayed fare and pricing settings are locked when you confirm. Subsequent fleet pricing updates do not alter an already-confirmed booking. By confirming, you agree to pay the driver upon trip completion in cash or by another agreed direct payment method.

2. Return Trips & Excess Waiting Charges

For return trips, the agreed waiting duration and the rate for exceeding it are clearly displayed before booking confirmation. If you exceed the agreed waiting time, an additional charge is accumulated using the locked waiting rate. The app displays this accumulating fee while the driver waits. You agree to pay the confirmed fare plus this excess charge upon return completion. Drivers may not add any arbitrary off-app markups.

3. Pickup Location & Accuracy

With permission, LaynRider requests location when needed for foreground service-area checks or when you select "Use Current Location" for a pickup. It does not track riders in the background. Pickup and destination coordinates selected for a booking are stored with that trip. You are responsible for ensuring your pickup pin is accurate and situated in a safe, accessible area for the driver to stop.

4. Passenger Conduct & Safety

Riders must treat drivers and their vehicles with care and respect. Damaging a driver's vehicle, carrying illegal substances, or engaging in harassing or aggressive behaviour will result in immediate permanent account suspension across all Digilayn platforms.

5. Community Use & Assumption of Risk

LaynRider is a connection platform for the Poortjie community, not a transport carrier. Digilayn does not employ drivers and does not verify the real identity of any rider or driver — names, photos, and phone numbers are self-provided and unverified. You use LaynRider at your own risk and are solely responsible for deciding whom to ride with; you should only accept rides from drivers you personally know and trust. Digilayn is not liable for user conduct or events occurring during a trip.

6. Cancellations

If you must cancel a ride, please cancel promptly through the app before the driver arrives to avoid unnecessary travel and fuel costs for the driver.

Driver Agreement

Terms for LaynDriver Partners

1. Independent Fleet Operator Status

Drivers operate as independent transport providers. Digilayn provides technology software and is not an employer or transport carrier. You retain full control over when you choose to go online, go offline, and accept ride requests.

2. Licensing, Roadworthiness & Legal Compliance

You represent and warrant that you hold a valid driver's license (with appropriate Professional Driving Permit / PrDP if applicable), that your vehicle is fully licensed and roadworthy, and that you comply with all South African National Road Traffic laws.

3. Live Location Sharing

While toggled to "Online" or during an assigned booking, LaynDriver requires background and foreground location access. This allows the dispatch radar to broadcast your position to nearby riders and compute accurate pickup routes and ETAs.

4. Democratic Fare Governance

Digilayn introduced the distance-based pricing curve. Eligible drivers vote on proposed fleet pricing changes for their vehicle type, including changes to the curve steepness. Approved pricing changes apply to new bookings; already-confirmed bookings retain their original fare and pricing settings. Drivers must charge only the confirmed fare plus the disclosed excess-waiting charge where applicable, and may not add off-app markups.

5. Road Liability & Disclaimers

Digilayn is not liable for traffic violations, road accidents, vehicle wear and tear, or third-party claims arising from vehicle operations. Drivers are required to maintain appropriate motor vehicle insurance.

Digilayn Platform Privacy Addendum

LaynFleet is powered by the unified Digilayn backend. Your universal account credentials, profile custody, strict 18+ eligibility, security safeguards, and statutory rights are governed by the parent Digilayn Privacy Policy under POPIA. This Addendum details the operational data practices specific to ride dispatch, real-time location sharing, and trip records.

⚠️ Poortjie Community Platform — Used at Your Own Risk

  • • LaynFleet is built for the Poortjie community, where riders and drivers largely know one another to arrange local trips.
  • • We do not verify the real identity of any user. Names, photos, and phone numbers are self-provided and unverified against official government registers.
  • • Riders and drivers use LaynFleet at their own risk. You should only accept or offer rides to and from people you personally know and trust.
1. Information Collected in LaynFleet
🧑 LaynRider

Rider Information

  • Account Identity: Display name, registered email, profile photo, and mobile phone number (stored to allow matched drivers to contact you; not verified via SMS).
  • Trip & Booking Data: Requested pickup location, destination, road-route distance, fare quotation, ride status, and timestamps.
  • On-Demand Foreground Location: With permission, LaynRider requests location when needed in the foreground to check service-area coverage or set a pickup pin. It does not run background rider tracking.
  • Ratings & Feedback: Voluntary ratings (1–5 stars) and optional feedback submitted after completed trips.
🚗 LaynDriver

Driver Information

  • Driver Profile & Vehicle: Full name, phone number, vehicle details (make, model, colour, plate registration), and documentation status.
  • Live Location Tracking (Foreground & Background): While toggled to "Online" or executing an active trip, LaynDriver collects continuous GPS location to broadcast vehicle availability on radar and calculate accurate arrival times.
  • Fleet Performance: Trip completion records, rider ratings, acceptance rates, and voting records on fleet pricing curve adjustments.
📱 Technical Infrastructure

Device & Telemetry Data

  • Device Registry: Recording active device sessions to keep accounts secure and allow single-device preferences.
  • Push Tokens: Cryptographic tokens from Firebase Cloud Messaging (or Huawei Push) to deliver trip dispatch alerts.
  • Maps & SDK Telemetry: Google Maps and Places SDKs (or Huawei Map Kit) collect technical telemetry, IP addresses, and interaction metrics necessary to render maps and route navigation.
2. Data Sharing Between Riders & Drivers

When a rider and driver are matched for a trip, personal details are shared exclusively to coordinate pickup and execution:

  • The driver sees the rider's name, profile photo, rating, contact phone number, and pickup location pin, and can call the rider directly from the app.
  • The rider sees the driver's name, profile photo, vehicle make, model, colour, registration plate number, live GPS location, and phone number, and can call the driver directly.

Riders can also view driver profiles and community ratings before booking. Submitted driver reviews and star ratings are visible within the platform to assist community transparency.

3. Transport Data Retention & Trip Records
📁

Universal account deletion is managed centrally under the parent Digilayn Privacy Policy. When a user account is deleted or when you stop using LaynFleet, transport-specific records are handled as follows:

Rider Trip Data

When a rider account is deleted through the universal Digilayn deletion process, rider-created bookings and associated trip pickup/dropoff logs are permanently removed from active databases.

Driver Booking History & Audit Logs

When a driver profile is deleted, personal driver details and vehicle registrations are permanently removed. However, completed trip records, dispatch timestamps, and fare logs are retained unchanged in an immutable audit state for dispatch accounting, road safety compliance, and financial verification.

Universal Account Deletion

To permanently delete your universal Digilayn Account and erase your personal data across all connected platforms, see Section 6 of the Digilayn Privacy Policy.
