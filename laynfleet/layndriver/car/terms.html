Terms for adding a vehicle

These terms explain what you agree to when you submit a vehicle through LaynDriver. They apply to every vehicle type, including a Private Car.

Please read before submitting

By submitting a vehicle, you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms. They supplement the LaynFleet Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. These terms govern vehicle submissions and listings; the LaynFleet Terms govern other use of the platform. Your rights under applicable law are not limited by this wording.

1. How vehicle types may be used

A Private Car is submitted for use in LaynFleet rides, including Quick Ride and Scheduled Ride requests. It is not a public vehicle-directory listing: its details are visible to the requester for a ride or request you explicitly accept or approve, as needed for that interaction. Other vehicle types may be used for ride requests where supported and may also appear in the LaynRider vehicle directory for advertising and customer enquiries. Directory advertising is separate from accepting a ride request: a customer who contacts you has not booked a ride, and you have not agreed to provide transport until you and the customer make that arrangement through the applicable LaynFleet flow or directly with each other.

2. Information you submit and listing visibility

The driver application can include your signed-in email, name, phone number and profile photo, together with your vehicle type, any applicable Bakkie subtype, make, model, colour, licence plate and passenger-seat capacity. Your Driver profile may separately request a photo of your driving licence as application evidence. You must provide accurate information and have the right or permission to submit it.

A non-private vehicle listing may display the vehicle type, applicable subtype, make, model, colour and passenger capacity. The licence plate, driving-licence image, email address and private application evidence are not displayed in the public vehicle directory. If a customer chooses WhatsApp or direct call, the contact option may share your phone number with that customer and involve the relevant third-party service. The listing and contact screen will identify the information shown and the contact method before the customer proceeds. See the Privacy Policy for details about personal information handled by LaynFleet.

You confirm that you are authorised to publish the submitted vehicle details and to receive customer enquiries about the vehicle. Do not submit another person’s information or advertise a vehicle without that person’s permission.

3. Your vehicle and driver obligations

You are responsible for ensuring that you and the vehicle meet all requirements that apply to the way the vehicle is used. This includes holding the appropriate valid driving licence and any required Professional Driving Permit (PrDP), operating or public-transport authorisation, vehicle licence, roadworthiness certification, insurance and other permits required by law.

Requirements can depend on the vehicle category and use. You must not accept or perform a trip, or make a vehicle available for a use, unless you are legally authorised and the vehicle is lawful and safe for that use. You are responsible for maintaining the vehicle, keeping documents current, following road-traffic and passenger-safety requirements, and promptly correcting information that becomes inaccurate.

4. Review by Digilayn is not a certification

Digilayn may review an application or information you submit to decide whether the platform requirements for an account or listing are met. Any application approval, listing, profile status or access to LaynFleet is not a roadworthiness inspection, legal opinion, guarantee of vehicle condition, or confirmation that your permits, licence, insurance or other documents are valid or sufficient. Digilayn does not independently certify that your vehicle is safe or legally authorised for every use.

You remain responsible for checking your own legal obligations and documents. Digilayn may restrict, remove or suspend an application, listing or account where information is inaccurate, a safety concern is reported, or platform rules or legal requirements may have been breached.

5. Customer arrangements, incidents and complaints

You are responsible for the transport service you provide, your conduct as a driver or owner, the condition and lawful operation of your vehicle, and the arrangements you make directly with a customer. You are responsible for addressing customer enquiries and for complying with applicable payment, safety, insurance and transport requirements.

If there is a safety concern, suspected misuse, inaccurate listing or serious dispute, contact Digilayn through the support details in the LaynFleet Terms. Digilayn may review reports and take action under its platform rules, but does not guarantee that it can resolve a private dispute or recover money or losses for either party. Nothing here limits rights or remedies provided by law.

6. Acceptance and changes

These terms apply when you submit a vehicle through LaynDriver. Digilayn may update them as the service or legal requirements change. Any updated terms will be presented for acceptance before they apply to a new vehicle submission. If you do not agree to an update, you may contact Digilayn about your account and vehicle listing.
