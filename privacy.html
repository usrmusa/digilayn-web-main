Digilayn Global Data & Privacy Framework

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Digilayn collects, uses, safeguards, and processes personal information across all digital services, web portals, and mobile applications powered by the Digilayn platform. We adhere strictly to South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Effective date: November 16, 2026 • Published by Musa Mgijima trading as Digilayn • Republic of South Africa.

🛡️ Privacy Principles in Plain English

  • ✓ Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, profile records, or activity logs to third-party brokers or advertisers.
  • ✓ Universal Digilayn Account: Digilayn Services share a unified backend and user database. Creating an account on any Digilayn Service registers a single universal account accessible across the ecosystem.
  • ✓ Strict 18+ Threshold: Digilayn Services and universal accounts are intended solely for individuals 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly process minors' personal data.
  • ✓ Right to Erasure: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your universal account and personal data across all Digilayn Services at any time.
  • ✓ Service Privacy Addenda: Feature-specific data practices (such as live driver GPS broadcast or customer delivery details) are governed by each respective service's privacy addendum alongside this global policy.

Section 2

Personal Information We Collect

Because Digilayn Services operate on a unified architecture, the personal information collected is categorized as follows:

2.1 Universal Account & Identity Data

When you register for a universal Digilayn Account through any Digilayn Service, we collect:

  • Your email address (used for authentication, account security, and verification);
  • Your login authentication credentials (securely hashed passwords or federated third-party identity tokens like Google Sign-In);
  • Your system-generated Unique User Identifier (UID);
  • A contact mobile phone number (used for operational dispatch and cross-user communications; stored as provided without mandatory SMS verification).

2.2 Profile Information

Information you voluntarily provide to complete your profile, including your display name, profile photo, and core account preferences. This profile information resides in our centralized database and is recognized across Digilayn Services you access.

2.3 Device & Technical Telemetry

When accessing Digilayn Services, technical information is automatically processed to secure your session and ensure proper platform functioning:

  • Device Registry: Operating system, hardware model, active device sessions, and sign-in status (to prevent unauthorized concurrent sessions);
  • Push Notification Tokens: Cryptographic tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging or Huawei Push Kit) to route necessary operational updates;
  • Diagnostic Logs: Server access timestamps, anonymized error telemetry, and security audit logs. We never log passwords, financial tokens, or secrets.

2.4 Service-Specific Data

Individual applications may request additional contextual data necessary for their specific features (such as foreground location for ride pickup or delivery street addresses for retail orders). Such data is collected and processed in accordance with that specific service's privacy addendum in conjunction with this policy.

Section 3

Lawful Basis for Processing (POPIA)

Under Section 11 of POPIA, Digilayn only processes personal information where a recognized lawful justification exists:

1. Consent

Freely given, informed consent provided when you register your universal Digilayn Account and accept these data terms.

2. Contractual Necessity

Processing required to create your universal identity, maintain your session, and deliver the services you explicitly request.

3. Legitimate Interests

Securing our shared database, preventing cross-platform fraud, and defending against unauthorized tampering.

4. Statutory Compliance

Retaining records where mandated by South African tax, accounting, or criminal justice legislation.

Section 4

Purpose of Processing

We use personal information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Universal Authentication: Verifying your identity and enabling seamless sign-in across any Digilayn-powered website, portal, or app;
  • Cross-Platform Security: Monitoring authentication attempts to prevent credential theft, brute-force attacks, and unauthorized database access;
  • Transactional Communications: Sending essential notifications, security alerts, password resets, and account status messages;
  • Dispute Resolution: Investigating reported platform abuse, resolving cross-platform violations, and enforcing the Digilayn Terms of Use;
  • Infrastructure Stability: Monitoring server load, debugging application errors, and maintaining high availability across our cloud systems.
Section 5

Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

5.1 No Sale or Commercial Exploitation

Digilayn maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against data selling. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal details to advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing intermediaries.

5.2 Cloud Infrastructure & Technical Operators

To deliver reliable digital services, Digilayn contracts with industry-standard cloud operators who act as Operators under POPIA:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Provides identity authentication, cloud hosting, distributed database storage (Firestore), and push notification routing;
  • Huawei Mobile Services (HMS): Provides push notification routing and map rendering on supported Huawei devices.

These operators process technical requests strictly on our instructions and subject to confidentiality and security obligations.

5.3 Legal Disclosures

We may disclose personal information only when legally compelled to do so by a valid court order, warrant, subpoena, or statutory directive issued by a competent South African law enforcement or regulatory authority.

Section 8

Children's Privacy (Strict 18+ Rule)

Digilayn strictly prohibits the creation of accounts or the submission of personal information by individuals under the age of 18 (eighteen) years. Digilayn Services are designed and intended exclusively for adult users possessing full contractual capacity.

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors. If we discover or are notified that an account belongs to a minor under 18, we will take immediate steps to terminate the account and purge all associated personal data from our systems.
