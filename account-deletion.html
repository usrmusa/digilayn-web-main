Account & Data Governance

Delete Your Account & Data

Digilayn provides full ownership and transparency over your personal information. This portal allows you to manage the permanent deletion of your account and personal data across all connected applications and services.

Compliance with South Africa's POPIA • Google Play Account Deletion Requirement • Single Universal Identity.

🛡️ Understanding Universal Deletion

  • ✓ Single Universal Account: Digilayn operates a shared database across all services. Deleting your universal account revokes your sign-in credentials across all Digilayn applications simultaneously.
  • ✓ Immediate Erasure: When confirmed, your authentication credentials, personal profile, device records, and user data are purged from active systems.
  • ⚠ Statutory Record Retention: In compliance with South African tax and transport regulations, completed transaction receipts and historical dispatch audit logs are retained in an immutable state for auditing and accounting compliance.

Option 2

LaynDriver Partners: Profile & Account Deletion

Drivers can request profile and account deletion through the app or by direct email:

  • In-App Request: Open More → Security Hub → Deregister in LaynDriver to submit a deletion request. This suspends the account pending manager processing.
  • Email Request: Email info@digilayn.co.za from your registered address.
  • Data Handled: Manager deletion permanently removes your driver profile, vehicle registrations, driver documentation, and device records. Completed booking records are retained unchanged for dispatch accounting and audit compliance.
